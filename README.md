# National Insurance Calculator UK — How Much NI Do I Pay?

Reference notes for the UK tax year **2026/27**, computed from HMRC's
published rates rather than copied from another site.

## How it works

Employee National Insurance is 8% on earnings between £12,570 and £50,270, then 2% above that.

The rate **falls** at the top, which is the opposite shape to Income Tax.

The rule that catches people out: NI is assessed on **each pay period separately** and closed. Income Tax under PAYE is cumulative and corrects itself; NI does not. A bonus month is charged as though that pay were your new normal, and the extra is never refunded.

## What this does not cover

Category letter matters. B, I and E pay the reduced 1.85% rate; C, S, K and X pay nothing at State Pension age; J, L, Z and D pay only the 2% upper rate. Letters like H and M change what your employer pays, not you.

## Source and tax year

Figures are for the 2026/27 tax year, which runs 6 April 2026
to 5 April 2027, and were checked against
[gov.uk](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/rates-and-thresholds-for-employers-2026-to-2027) on 28 August 2026.

Rates change every April, and often at a Budget. Check the tax year a figure
belongs to before relying on it.
